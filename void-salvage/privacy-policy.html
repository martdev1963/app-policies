Short version: Void Salvage is offline-first. In the current build, progress is saved only on your device. We do not require an account, and we do not collect personal information, analytics, or advertising identifiers.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Void Salvage (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information. The App is developed and published as an independent project.

Contact for privacy questions: YOUR_EMAIL@example.com

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information in the current build of the App.

Specifically, the App does not:

  • Require you to create an account or sign in
  • Collect your name, email address, phone number, or payment details
  • Use analytics or crash-reporting services that send data to us
  • Show live advertisements or use advertising SDKs
  • Access your contacts, photos, microphone, or location
  • Share data with third parties for advertising or profiling

3. Data stored on your device

The App stores game progress locally on your device, such as:

  • Unlocked ships, contracts, and hangar progression
  • Scrap, inventory items, and related run meta
  • Settings you choose in the App (for example, audio preferences)

This data stays on your device. Uninstalling the App, clearing app storage, or wiping the device may delete it. We cannot restore local progress from our side because we do not keep a copy on our servers.

4. Internet access

Core gameplay is designed to work offline. The current build does not require a network connection to play contracts or save progress. If the operating system or store client uses the network for installation, updates, or license checks, that is handled by the platform (for example Google Play), not by Void Salvage servers.

5. Children

The App is a general-audience arcade game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the current build does not collect personal data at all, we do not process children’s personal information.

6. Third-party services

The current build does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or cloud save providers.

Distribution platforms (such as Google Play) have their own privacy practices when you download or update the App. Those practices are governed by the platform’s policies, not this document.

7. Future changes (ads and in-app purchases)

We may later add features such as:

  • Rewarded or interstitial advertisements (for example via Google AdMob)
  • Optional in-app purchases through the platform store

If we do, those services may process device identifiers or purchase-related data under their own privacy policies. We will update this Privacy Policy and Play Console Data safety disclosures before enabling live ads or billing, and we will revise the “Last updated” date above.

8. Your choices

  • You can stop using the App at any time by uninstalling it.
  • You can clear local App data through your device settings.
  • Because we do not hold personal data on our servers, there is no account profile for us to export or delete remotely.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
